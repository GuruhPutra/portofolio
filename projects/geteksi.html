Project Description

This project involved the collaborative design of an Instagram feed for the GETEKSI event as a visual communication and promotional medium, supporting three key phases: pre-event promotion, real-time event updates, and post-event documentation. The design process utilized Adobe Photoshop as the primary tool for visual composition, layout arrangement, and maintaining visual consistency across posts, with careful attention to color harmony, typography, and information hierarchy to ensure clarity and cohesion. The primary objective was to present event information in a clear and engaging manner while reinforcing a consistent visual identity, and the resulting increase in views demonstrated that the visual strategy effectively captured audience attention and supported the event’s promotional goals.

Project Reflection

The GETEKSI project focused on creating a cohesive Instagram feed design to serve as a promotional and documentation tool for the event. The project was carried out in a team-based environment, where I contributed to the design process by developing visual layouts, refining design elements using Adobe Photoshop, and ensuring consistency across multiple feed posts. The designs were adapted to different event phases, requiring flexibility while maintaining a unified visual identity.

This project highlighted the strategic role of Instagram feed design in shaping audience perception of an event’s professionalism and quality. The observed increase in views demonstrated that consistent visual design and clear information delivery can significantly enhance audience engagement. Additionally, the collaborative nature of the project emphasized the importance of teamwork, shared design direction, and effective visual communication. The experience reinforced my understanding that social media design is not solely about aesthetics, but also about conveying messages efficiently and influencing audience response.

Moving forward, I plan to further develop my skills in social media design by incorporating more data-driven approaches, such as analyzing engagement metrics and audience behavior. I also aim to explore stronger visual storytelling techniques to enhance content impact across different event stages. The GETEKSI project serves as a valuable foundation for my continued growth in digital and social media design, both in academic settings and future professional projects.
