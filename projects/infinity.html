Project Description

This project involved the design of an Instagram feed for the INFINITY event, created to support event promotion and digital communication through social media. The feed was developed to convey the event’s theme, philosophy, and core messages using a cohesive visual identity. Particular attention was given to visual consistency, information hierarchy, and layout structure to ensure a professional and engaging representation of the INFINITY event on Instagram.

Project Reflection

The INFINITY project focused on designing a visually structured Instagram feed to serve as a promotional and informational medium for the event. The design process included planning the content sequence, creating visual compositions, and aligning individual posts to form a unified feed. Each design element was developed to reflect the event’s concept while maintaining clarity and visual harmony across all posts.

This project highlighted the strategic role of Instagram feed design in shaping audience perception and strengthening event branding. A consistent and well-organized feed enhances professionalism, improves message clarity, and supports effective visual storytelling. Through this experience, I gained insight into the importance of designing social media content holistically rather than treating each post as an isolated visual element.

Moving forward, I plan to further refine my social media and event design skills by adopting more audience-centered and performance-driven approaches. I aim to explore content evaluation methods such as engagement analysis to inform future design decisions. The INFINITY project serves as a foundation for applying design principles more strategically in future academic and professional projects related to digital event promotion.
