Project Description

This project involved the design and management of an Instagram feed for HIMAPRODI TI, developed as part of the responsibilities of the KOMINFO division. The feed served as an official digital communication platform for delivering organizational information, promoting activities, and commemorating national and institutional events. The design focused on maintaining visual consistency, clear information hierarchy, and alignment with the organization’s identity to ensure effective and professional communication with the audience.

Project Reflection

As a member of the KOMINFO division, I contributed to the design of Instagram feed content for HIMAPRODI TI. My responsibilities included creating visual layouts, arranging content structure, and ensuring consistency in color, typography, and overall design across posts. The feed was used to communicate organizational announcements, event-related information, and commemorative content through Instagram as the primary social media platform.

This project helped me understand the strategic role of social media design in shaping the public image and credibility of an organization. A well-designed and consistent Instagram feed enhances audience trust and engagement while improving the clarity of information delivery. Working within the KOMINFO division also strengthened my awareness of how visual design functions as a key communication tool, requiring precision, consistency, and alignment with organizational goals.

Moving forward, I plan to further develop my skills in social media design by adopting more audience-centered and data-informed approaches. I aim to enhance my ability to analyze engagement metrics to evaluate content effectiveness and refine future designs. This project provides a strong foundation for my continued growth in visual communication and digital media design within both academic and professional organizational contexts.
